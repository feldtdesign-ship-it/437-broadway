Traffic Cam Archive

Per clipprice on checkout

Sells stored video from the DOT cameras, roughly the last 90 to 150 days. One weekday and one Saturday a month back to June would give a summer baseline to count from. Coverage of the Canal St camera is not confirmed on their map.

Free, forward-looking

Snapshot job on the camera

$0about 1 GB a year

A scheduled task saves a frame from the Canal St camera every ten minutes. Around 53,000 frames a year. A simple detector counts heads in the crosswalk later. Owned data, but the first full season takes a season to collect, and operators re-aim the lens.
